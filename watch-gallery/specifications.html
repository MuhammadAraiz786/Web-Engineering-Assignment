Know the essentials

Understanding Watch Movements

Three common movement categories use different mechanisms to keep time.

01 / Mechanical

Automatic

An automatic watch uses a mainspring and a mechanical movement that can be wound by wrist motion. It typically needs no battery but may require regular servicing.

02 / Electronic

Quartz

A quartz movement uses an electronic circuit regulated by a quartz crystal. Many models are battery-powered and valued for practical timekeeping.

03 / Connected

Smart

A smartwatch uses electronic hardware and software to provide a digital display and model-dependent connected features.
